# Baca Komik (Read Comics)

Search for and read manga, manhwa, or manhua from the available sources. You start by choosing a source or All Sources, searching for a title, then opening a chapter.

## Getting started reading

1. Open the **Baca Komik** card and log in to Nexora if asked.
2. Wait for the reader page to finish loading.
3. In **Sumber** (Source), choose **All Sources** or a specific source.
4. Fill in the **Cari manga, manhwa, atau manhua** (Search manga, manhwa, or manhua) field, then press the search button.
5. Open the matching comic card.
6. Read the details and choose a chapter.
7. Wait for the chapter's image pages to load, then read by scrolling.
8. Use the available chapter navigation to continue or go back.

The same title may be available from several sources with different chapter lists. Make sure the source and the title are correct.

## All Sources and filters

**All Sources** tries searching across the collection of available sources. One failing source doesn't always mean all sources failed. Watch the loading status or progress.

**Filter** opens options for comic type, status, or other options the source actually provides. **Manga**, **Manhwa**, and **Manhua** are types of work; **Ongoing** means still continuing and **Tamat** means finished, according to the source's data.

Not every source provides the same filter data. If there are too few results, remove some filters and try a simpler title.

## Continuing your reading

The **Riwayat** (History) tab helps you reopen things you've read before. Choose the matching entry to continue at the chapter or position available.

Use the comic list or back button to choose another title. Inside the reader, tap the available controls if the toolbar is hidden to make the images wider.

## If images or chapters have problems

- Wait for pages that are still loading; long chapters load many images.
- If one source has problems, try another source that offers that title.
- If you're asked to log in or need special access, follow Nexora's settings.
- If a chapter is missing, don't assume the whole comic is unavailable; the list follows the source.
- A history entry doesn't mean all images have been downloaded for offline reading.

The audited version of the reader focuses on reading from sources; it doesn't automatically translate all comic images. For classic text works with translation options, see [World Classics Reader](worldclassics.md).
