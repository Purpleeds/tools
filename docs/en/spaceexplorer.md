# Space Explorer

Explore NASA space data and images through four menus: APOD, Mars, Asteroid, and space weather.

## Choose a menu

| Menu | What you see | How to start |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **APOD** | Astronomy Picture of the Day, which may be an image or other media. | Choose an available date and open the explanation. |
| **Mars** | A gallery related to Mars missions. | Choose an available mission and browse the result pages. |
| **Asteroid** | Information on near-Earth objects for a given date. | Choose a date, then read the distance, estimated size, and classification. |
| **Cuaca** (Weather) | Space weather events. | Choose an available range or filter and read the event times. |

## How to use it

1. Open Space Explorer.
2. Choose a menu.
3. Fill in the date or the options shown.
4. Wait for the data to finish loading.
5. Open a card to see details and media.
6. Use **Refresh data** if needed.
7. Use the favorite marker if you want to save a pick in the browser.

Example: choose APOD and a date to read the science story behind that day's media. Not every day is a photo, so don't assume different media means an error.

## Reading the information correctly

**Near Earth** means the object belongs to the near-Earth object group according to the source data, not that it will hit Earth.

Asteroid classifications and estimated diameters follow NASA data, which may be updated. Distances must be read together with their units. The weather in this menu is **space** weather, not a rain forecast for your city.

## Issues

If media doesn't appear, open the available source details and check your connection. If a date has no data, try another supported date. The Mars gallery follows the available mission data; it's not a live camera feed from a rover.

For Indonesian weather and earthquake information, use [BMKG Indonesia](bmkg.md).
