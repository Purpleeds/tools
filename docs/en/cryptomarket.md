# Crypto Market Scanner

Browse crypto asset data, price changes, and analysis across several time ranges. It's meant to help you read data, not to give certainty about the next price.

## Getting started

1. Choose **Currency: IDR** or **USD**.
2. Choose the number of assets: Top 10, Top 25, or Top 50.
3. Use the search field for a name or a symbol such as BTC.
4. Sort by Market Cap, Volume, Top Gainer, Top Loser, or Harga (Price).
5. Open the asset you want to look at more closely.
6. Read the chart and the Analisis Multi-Timeframe (Multi-Timeframe Analysis) section.
7. Use **Refresh** if you need to check the data again.

## Understanding the numbers

| Term | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Harga** (Price) | The asset's value in the chosen currency. |
| **Market Cap** | Market value based on the capitalization data the provider gives. |
| **Volume** | The value of trading activity during the data period shown. |
| **Top Gainer / Top Loser** | Sorting by rise or fall over the related period. |
| **Candle / timeframe** | A summary of price movement over a given time range. |
| **Mikro / makro** (Micro / macro) | A comparison of direction over a shorter or longer range. |

Choose the matching time range when comparing indicators. A 15-minute chart and a 1-hour chart can show different conditions without either one being necessarily wrong.

## Example

To read Bitcoin in rupiah, choose IDR, search for BTC, then open its analysis. Check whether the chart, the 24-hour change, and the indicators refer to the same period before drawing conclusions.

## Limits and issues

Data may be cached and is subject to the provider's quota. Refreshing repeatedly doesn't guarantee second-by-second fresher prices. If part of the analysis isn't available, read its status; don't treat a missing number as zero.

Indicators, scores, and risk summaries are not a guarantee of profit or automatic trading instructions. This tool doesn't buy or sell assets for you.
