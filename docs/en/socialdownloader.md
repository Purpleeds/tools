# Social Downloader

Fetches media from TikTok or Instagram links, then offers the download options found at the source.

## How to use it

1. In TikTok or Instagram, copy the link of the post you want.
2. Open Social Downloader.
3. Paste it into **Link media** (Media link) or use the **Paste** button.
4. Press **Get Media**.
5. Wait for the information and media list to appear.
6. Choose the video, audio, or image that's actually available in the result.
7. Open or download it through that option.

If the browser doesn't allow Paste, long-press the field and choose Tempel (Paste).

## Choosing a result

The quality shown follows the source. An HD or Best Quality label doesn't turn a low-resolution video into a new HD one.

One post can give several media items or quality options. Separate audio can only be fetched if the service actually returns it; don't assume every post has the same options.

## If nothing is found

Check that the link is from TikTok or Instagram and is still accessible. Deleted, restricted, or private posts may be unavailable. Don't paste links from other platforms assuming this tool supports every site.

Downloads come from the source/CDN. If it opens as a media tab, use the browser's save option if available. Use media that's yours or that you're actually allowed to use.
