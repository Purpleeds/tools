# Alight Motion Premium 1 Tahun (1 Year)

A page for requesting a magic link and passing an Alight Motion Premium application to the provider service that Nexora connects to. The catalog name says **1 Year**, but subscription status still has to be confirmed from the Alight Motion account.

## Stages on the page

1. **Request Magic Link:** enter an email and request a link.
2. **Apply Premium:** enter the email and the magic link to be passed on to the provider.

Access and availability depend on Nexora's settings and the provider's service. Opening the page is not the same as having a subscription.

## How to read the flow

1. Log in to Nexora and open the tool if your access allows it.
2. Check the service status at the top.
3. Enter your Alight Motion email in the Request Magic Link stage.
4. Press the button once, wait for the message, then check your inbox and spam.
5. If you continue the application through the provider, make sure the second email is the same and that the link you use came from that email.
6. Read the application result message.
7. Check the subscription status through the official Alight Motion app or service to confirm a legitimate entitlement.

A **magic link** is a sensitive authentication link. The form clears it after use; keep it only for the process you're carrying out.

## Tell three kinds of status apart

| Status | What it proves |
| --- | --- |
| Magic link sent, according to the provider | The send request was accepted; check the actual email. |
| Application processed, according to the provider | The provider responded to that request. |
| Subscription actually active | Requires checking the legitimate subscription/entitlement status on the Alight Motion account. |

A gateway success message is not a substitute for proof of account status. This guide does not claim that a one-year duration is definitely applied or that the provider's service is always available.

## If you get stuck

Statuses such as **Provider belum diverifikasi** (Provider not yet verified), daily quota exhausted, rate limit, or authentication failure should be read according to their message. Nexora's anti-spam delay is not always the same as the provider's quota recovery time.

If requesting the link times out, check your email first. **Bersihkan Form** (Clear Form) deletes the input. Repeating many times doesn't prove activation and can extend the restriction.

For sending and verifying authentication without treating it as a Premium application, see [AM Send & Verif](amsendverif.md).
