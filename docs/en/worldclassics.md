# World Classics Reader

Read classic literature from Chinese, Japanese, and Korean sources, with the option of the original text or an Indonesian translation where available.

## Finding and opening a work

1. Choose **Sumber: China, Jepang, atau Korea** (Source: China, Japan, or Korea).
2. Enter the title of the work.
3. Press **Cari karya** (Find works).
4. Open the matching result, then choose a chapter.
5. Use **Muat bab lainnya** (Load more chapters) if the visible list isn't complete.
6. Read the chapter in the view you want.

If a romanized title doesn't find anything, try the original title used by the source. Results follow the source's collection, not every novel in the world.

## Views and translation

| Option | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Original** | The text in the source language. |
| **Indonesia** | The Indonesian translation, if already available. |
| **Bilingual** | The original text and the translation, to read together. |
| **Literal** | A translation that stays closer to the source's structure. |
| **Natural** | A translation that reads more smoothly. |
| **Novel** | Translation language in a narrative style. |

Choose a translation mode, then **Terjemahkan bab** (Translate chapter) if the Indonesian version isn't available yet. Just switching the view to Indonesia doesn't mean the whole work has been translated.

Example: open a chapter, try Natural, wait for the translation, then use Bilingual to compare parts that are hard to understand.

## Reading comfort

Adjust **Ukuran teks** (Text size) if it's too small on your phone. Use **Bab sebelumnya / Bab berikutnya** (Previous chapter / Next chapter) to move around. **Simpan bookmark** (Save bookmark) marks your reading so it's easier to continue through Continue Reading & Bookmark.

A bookmark isn't a download of the whole book. Don't assume every chapter is automatically available offline just because you've opened it.

## If there are issues

If the search results are empty, check the source and the title's spelling. If the translation isn't ready or the service is limiting requests, the original text is still a different thing from the translated result. Check names, cultural terms, and important sentences against the original text if accuracy matters.
